# Session recap

A page the reader opens to see where a unit of work stands, written to be
read in a minute. Render it as a polished page: it is the artifact a session
ends with.

1. **Bottom line.** The lede, against the goal: met, partly met, or moved, in
   one sentence.
2. **Goal.** The current `goal`, who set it, and what done means. When the goal
   moved during the work, say so in one line; don't trace each revision.
3. **Outcomes.** One card per `outcome`, each with its link: what now holds and
   why it matters. Lead with the ones the goal asked for.
4. **Decisions.** Each `decision` as its choice and the reason, one line each.
   A decision that supersedes earlier work says what it replaced in a clause,
   not a section.
5. **What's left.** Open `action` facts with an owner and the next step, and
   any contradiction still open. When nothing is left, say so.

Leave out every fact marked `superseded by`, and every fact that only narrates
how the work went. A reader who wants the story follows the link to the
retrospective, when one exists.
