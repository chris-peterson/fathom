fathom is keeping {record} (kind `session`) for this unit of work. Keep it the
way a good lede is kept: fewer facts, each one an outcome.

Between turns, record only what changes where the work stands, with
`/fathom:record` and one `fathom add` per batch:

- the `goal`, when it is set or moves (one key, revised as a new row);
- an `outcome`: something landed, shipped, fixed, learned, or ruled out, stated
  as the result, with its link;
- a `decision`, with the choice and the reason;
- an `action` left open, with its owner.

Never record the path: attempts, retries, edits, test runs, or how a result was
reached. The transcript already holds every turn. Most turns record nothing.
When a fact moves the bottom line, revise the lede and say so in one line.
